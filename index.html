What TechBridge is

TechBridge is the learning and internship initiative of Baselink Services Limited. We exist to close the gap between classroom knowledge and the practical work that real teams do.

  • What we offer Practical programs in Data Analytics and Web Development, and a 30-day practical internship.
  • Who it is for Interns and learners who want hands-on experience, whatever stage they are at.
  • Why join You learn by doing project-based work, and strong performance can earn you a recommendation letter.
